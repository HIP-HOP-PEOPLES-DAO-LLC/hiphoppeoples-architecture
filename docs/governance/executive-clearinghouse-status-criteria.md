## 1. Purpose

This document defines the formal status criteria, review cadence, evidence requirements, and control rules used by the Executive Clearinghouse to maintain the Hip Hop Peoples / MAEG Ecosystem History Register.

The purpose is to prevent informal claims, old marketing language, or unverified metrics from being treated as institutional facts before proper source evidence is attached.

This document supports the ecosystem’s governing standard:

> Creating a documented evidence trail that institutions can review, underwrite, verify, and respond to through their existing compliance processes.

---

## 2. Scope

This status criteria framework applies to all entries in HHP-HIST-REG-001, including:

- Core programs
- Economic lanes
- Media projects
- Technology initiatives
- Youth and family activities
- Peer support and workforce programs
- Production apprenticeships
- Food systems activity
- Public trust / justice navigation lanes
- Membership and marketplace products
- Site-based operating nodes
- Future Executive Clearinghouse records

This document does not store participant files, clinical records, payroll records, protected health information, youth records, court/case records, or sensitive eligibility documents.

## 3. Status Categories

The Executive Clearinghouse uses four primary statuses:

| Status | Definition | Minimum Evidence Required |
|---|---|---|
| Active | The activity is currently operating, tracked, governed, or connected to a live system, SOP, dashboard, file lane, registration/payment flow, partner file, or operating record. | At least one current source record tied to the correct file lane. |
| Planning | The activity is designed, proposed, or being prepared but is not yet fully operating. | Blueprint, concept note, draft SOP, budget, site plan, partner discussion, or planning file. |
| Pending Verification | The activity, metric, claim, enrollment status, billing target, budget, revenue figure, or compliance status has been reported but lacks sufficient source evidence for external use. | Source evidence must be attached before use in grants, underwriting, lender packets, public claims, or partner due diligence. |
| Archived | The activity is historical and no longer active, but remains part of institutional memory. | Prior flyer, photo/video record, event note, correspondence, summary memo, or historical documentation. |

## 4. Status Criteria

### 4.1 Active

An entry may be marked **Active** only when at least one of the following exists:

- Active SOP or governance record
- Active Notion dashboard or controlled database
- Active website page with compliant language
- Active Stripe, registration, or intake flow
- Active grant, fiscal sponsor, or partner record
- Active project file, production file, or site file
- Active training, curriculum, or enrollment workflow
- Active meeting, approval, agreement, or operational record
- Active budget or ledger aligned with CIOS-REV-001

Active does not mean that every metric is verified. An activity may be active while individual metrics remain pending verification.

### 4.2 Planning

An entry may be marked **Planning** when the activity has a defined concept or operating intent but lacks full launch evidence.

Planning evidence may include:

- Blueprint
- Draft budget
- Draft SOP
- Concept note
- Program outline
- Site plan
- Form draft
- Internal memo
- Partner discussion record
- Pending application
- Pending bank/account setup
- Pending compliance checklist

Planning entries must not be represented as fully operational, licensed, funded, approved, credentialed, or revenue-producing unless source evidence supports that statement.

### 4.3 Pending Verification

An entry or metric must be marked **Pending Verification** when it is based on memory, discussion, reported figures, draft files, screenshots, early architecture, or unlinked documents.

The following must remain Pending Verification until source evidence is attached:

- Revenue figures
- Billing targets
- Medicaid or clinical billing claims
- Provider enrollment status
- Grant award or reimbursement status
- Production budget figures
- Qualified spend claims
- Employment or placement counts
- Cohort completion rates
- Retention metrics
- Youth program licensing claims
- Partnership agreements
- Bond, insurance, or underwriting status
- Bank facility, bridge loan, or LOC claims
- Fiscal sponsor approvals
- Public trust / bail agreement claims

### 4.4 Archived

An entry may be marked **Archived** when it is no longer operating but should remain in the institutional memory register.

Archived evidence may include:

- Flyers
- Photos
- Event records
- Partner correspondence
- Social posts captured as historical records
- Press or website archives
- Summary memos
- Closeout notes

Archived entries should not be deleted unless they are inaccurate, duplicative, unsafe, or legally inappropriate to preserve.

---

## 5. Status Upgrade Rule

No entry may move from **Pending Verification** to **Active** based on memory, conversation, informal notes, or marketing language alone.

A status upgrade requires:

1. Source evidence attached or linked.
2. Correct file lane identified.
3. Sensitive data removed or stored in the proper restricted location.
4. Cross-reference to the applicable governance or financial control.
5. Executive Clearinghouse review note.
6. Dated revision entry.

## 8. File-Lane Separation Rules

The Executive Clearinghouse must enforce file-lane separation across every update.

### 8.1 Prohibited in GitHub

The following must not be stored in GitHub:

- SSN
- Medical information
- PHI
- Minor/youth private records
- Payroll documents
- Government IDs
- Background records
- Court/case-specific records
- Full participant eligibility files
- Bank account numbers
- Payment-card details
- Clinical documentation
- Diagnosis or treatment plans

### 8.2 Correct Location for Sensitive Records

Sensitive records must be stored only in the proper restricted lane, such as:

- Secure credentialing folder
- Payroll system
- Clinical partner system
- Fiscal sponsor restricted file
- Grant compliance folder
- Youth restricted intake folder
- Legal/public trust restricted file
- Production payroll/HR folder

## 9. Executive Clearinghouse Weekly Review Cadence

### 9.1 Review Frequency

The Executive Clearinghouse should review the institutional memory register once per week.

**Recommended review window:** Friday closeout or Monday readiness review.

### 9.2 Weekly Review Agenda

Each review should include:

1. New entries added since last review.
2. Pending Verification items requiring evidence.
3. Status upgrade requests.
4. Metrics or financial claims needing CIOS-REV-001 support.
5. Workforce entries needing MAEG-WF-SOP-001 support.
6. Clinical or Medicaid-related entries needing firewall review.
7. Youth entries needing parent/guardian or safety review.
8. Underwriting entries needing Stripe/ADP memo support.
9. Governance entries needing CGC Resolution CIOS-BR-001 support.
10. CIOS-REV-001 reporting deadline check.
11. Revision log update.

### 9.3 Monthly Executive Sweep

Once per month, the Executive Clearinghouse should conduct a deeper sweep to identify:

- Duplicative entries
- Unsupported claims
- Outdated statuses
- Unlinked evidence
- Sensitive data exposure risk
- Dead links
- Missing file-lane labels
- Items that should be archived
- Items ready to move from Planning to Active
- Items that must remain Pending Verification
- GitHub contributors or file changes that should be reconciled against the Stripe & ADP Anchor Identification Memo where payment, payroll, or underwriting evidence is referenced
- Entries requiring CGC Resolution CIOS-BR-001 cross-reference due to governance, bridge-finance, or independent oversight implications

### 9.4 CIOS-REV-001 Sync

The weekly and monthly review cadence must be adjusted if CIOS-REV-001 establishes a stricter reporting deadline.

If a CIOS-REV-001 reporting deadline falls before the normal Friday closeout or Monday readiness review, the Executive Clearinghouse must complete revenue-related verification before that reporting deadline.

## 14. Governance Maintenance

### 14.1 Version Control Protocol

Updates to HHP-EC-STATUS-001 should follow a controlled GitHub workflow:

1. Draft update on a branch or direct controlled edit only when authorized.
2. Include a clear commit message stating the governance purpose.
3. Require review by the Operations Lead or designated Governance Reviewer before merger into the primary branch when protected-branch rules are available.
4. Update the version number and revision log for any substantive rule change.
5. Do not combine governance-rule changes with unrelated program-history edits in the same commit unless the change is an emergency correction.

Substantive rule changes include changes to status definitions, evidence standards, access rights, emergency override rules, archive retention, prohibited-use language, and required strategic cross-references.

### 14.2 Emergency Override

Emergency overrides are permitted only to prevent material harm, legal exposure, sensitive-data leakage, false external claims, safety issues, or institutional misrepresentation.

Emergency override authority may be exercised by:

- Operations Lead
- Governance Reviewer acting under CGC Resolution CIOS-BR-001 authority
- Authorized CGC role designated in a signed resolution

Emergency override actions may include:

- Moving an entry to Hold
- Reverting an unsafe claim
- Removing or redacting sensitive information
- Blocking external use of an entry
- Marking an entry Pending Verification
- Freezing a status upgrade until evidence is reviewed

Emergency overrides must be documented within 48 hours using the Decision Log or a governance note. No emergency override may be used to upgrade an unsupported claim to Active for external use.

### 14.3 New Reviewer Onboarding

New Executive Clearinghouse reviewers must be oriented to:

- The Source Evidence Matrix
- File-lane separation rules
- Prohibited GitHub content
- Pending Verification standards
- Clinical firewall language
- People’s Pass non-investment / non-employment controls
- MAEG-WF-SOP-001 workforce placement gates
- CIOS-REV-001 revenue and accrual standards
- CGC Resolution CIOS-BR-001 governance authority
- Stripe & ADP Anchor Identification Memo use limits

New reviewers should not approve status upgrades until they understand which evidence belongs in GitHub and which evidence must remain in restricted folders.

### 14.4 Technical Guardrails

The repository should adopt automation where practical.

Recommended GitHub guardrails:

- Pull request checklist requiring relevant cross-references.
- Labels such as `needs-CIOS-REV`, `needs-MAEG-WF-SOP`, `needs-CGC-resolution`, `needs-Stripe-ADP`, `clinical-firewall-review`, `youth-safety-review`, and `pending-verification`.
- GitHub Actions or PR templates that flag missing required cross-reference language.
- Branch protection for governance documents once repository operations mature.
- CODEOWNERS or reviewer rules for files under `docs/governance/` and `docs/history/`.

Automation should flag risk; it should not make final governance decisions without human review.

### 14.5 Archive Retention

Archived entries should remain in HHP-HIST-REG-001 or a linked archive for at least seven years unless legal counsel, governance authority, or a safety/privacy concern requires a different retention period.

Permanent deletion should be rare and must require one of the following:

- Duplicate record
- Clear factual error
- Sensitive-data exposure
- Legal or privacy risk
- Counsel or governance instruction
- Migration to a controlled off-chain or restricted archive with a retained index entry

If an item is moved off-chain or to restricted storage, the public/non-sensitive index should preserve:

- Entry title
- Archive date
- Reason for archive
- File lane
- Reviewer
- Restricted-location reference without exposing sensitive data

### 14.6 Stripe/ADP Contributor & Evidence Reconciliation

During the monthly sweep, any GitHub file or PR referencing payment, payroll, lender-safe evidence, W-2 hires, paid placements, or underwriting support must be reconciled against the Stripe & ADP Anchor Identification Memo or an equivalent approved evidence index.

This reconciliation is not a payroll audit. It confirms that public or GitHub-facing claims do not exceed the evidence available in the proper payment/payroll/underwriting lane.

## 15. Prohibited Review Outcomes

The Executive Clearinghouse must not:

- Upgrade status based on verbal confirmation only.
- Use GitHub as a storage location for restricted records.
- Treat People’s Pass membership as employment, investment, clinical access, or governance rights.
- Treat training enrollment as paid placement.
- Treat a draft budget as a verified budget.
- Treat planned site activity as licensed activity.
- Treat provider-aligned work as HHHI independent billing without enrollment proof.
- Treat bridge-loan targets as approved credit facilities.
- Treat a completion-bond package as an issued bond.
- Use emergency override authority to make an unsupported claim externally usable.

## 17. Final Control Statement

The Executive Clearinghouse exists to convert ecosystem memory into reviewable institutional evidence without overstating status, exposing sensitive records, or mixing file lanes.

Every update must answer five questions:

1. What is the claim?
2. What is the source evidence?
3. What is the correct file lane?
4. What governance control applies?
5. Is the entry safe for external use, or still pending verification?

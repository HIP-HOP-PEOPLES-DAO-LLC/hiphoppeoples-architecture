## 3. Access & Licensing Protocol

- BlackCoins NFT = licensed use right (not ownership)
- Soul-Bound Ministry ID + active Street Credit required for entry
- Stevie AI validates NFT + Street Credit before physical or digital access is granted
- All access logged with timestamp and blockchain hash

---

## 4. Daily / Weekly CGC Site Management Procedures

### Daily
- Stevie AI runs overnight compliance scan
- CGC reviews exception report (violations, occupancy, energy usage)
- Freedom Notes rewards issued for verified labor

### Weekly
- CGC physical site walk-through (Zone A1–C1)
- Update land improvement log in Trust ledger
- Reconcile Street Credit ledger with Freedom Notes issuance

### Monthly
- Full Stevie AI audit report generated
- NFT access rights reviewed
- PMA rule compliance certification

---

## 5. Emergency & Revocation Protocol

1. Stevie AI detects critical violation → immediate access suspension + alert to CGC
2. CGC votes on permanent revocation (DAO governance)
3. Trust legal team executes formal notice if needed

## 1. Purpose

This document records the planned **media and marketing layer** of the Hip Hop Peoples ecosystem, centered on **Hip Hop Peoples Universe** (also styled *Hip-hop Peoples Universe* / HHP Universe).

It exists so partners, builders, and operators can see how casting, membership identity, peer-support pathways, film production, and the gamified city experience fit together — without mixing marketing language into clinical, payroll, or compliance records.

**Status of this layer:** Planning (concept art, casting materials, and product mockups captured; not yet a live production system of record).

---

## 2. What Hip Hop Peoples Universe Is

Hip Hop Peoples Universe is the **public engagement surface** for culture, opportunity, and community identity across Eastern North Carolina (Greenville / Pitt County / 252 and beyond).

It is designed as:

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| **Community ID** | Member identity card (physical + digital) linking people to pathways |
| **City experience** | Gamified Greenville map with nodes, missions, XP, and rewards |
| **Media lane** | Film casting, studio content, and storytelling (e.g. *Married to the Hustle*) |
| **Pathway pillars** | Artist, workforce, peer support, legacy/trades, advocacy |
| **Partner gateways** | Upland virtual-city skills, Krafty Academy referrals, MAEG / T2F Media production |

**Tagline set in current creative:**

> One ID. Many pathways. Real people. Real opportunity. Real results.

**It is not:**

- A clinical EHR or Medicaid billing system  
- An investment, security, token, or NFT offering  
- A guarantee of employment, casting, or paid placement  
- A replacement for GitLab systems of record or HHHI credential verification controls

| Content type | Correct lane |

## 8. Build Implications (For Product / Web Teams)

When this layer moves from Planning toward Active:

1. **Front door** remains hiphoppeoples.com program navigation (Get Support / Build Skills / Create & Earn).  
2. **Universe app** should treat City XP as engagement state, with privacy review before storing resident profiles.  
3. **Community ID** issuance should map to People’s Pass membership rules and disclaimers.  
4. **Casting** stays on MAEG production intake, not mixed into peer-support case systems.  
5. **HHHI verification** stays behind credential gates; marketing QR codes must not collect PHI or youth records into public forms.

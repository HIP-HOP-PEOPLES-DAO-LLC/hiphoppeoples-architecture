# 2026 Governance Architecture

## Addendum A — District 05: Market & Asset Lab Governance Charter

**Effective status:** Draft / implementation control  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Maintained by:** Hip-Hop Peoples DAO LLC  
**System designation:** Powered by Stevie AI  
**Supervisory function:** Stevie AI Native Market Sentinel  
**Relationship to existing architecture:** This addendum supplements the Ecosystem Routing Map and Freedom Notes public policy. It does not amend those documents unless separately approved.

---

## A1. Purpose

District 05 — Market & Asset Lab exists to:

1. discover and monitor native digital-asset markets;
2. register proposed ecosystem assets;
3. classify assets before deployment;
4. preserve evidence supporting each classification;
5. separate non-financial participation systems from financial or investment-like instruments;
6. require Human Gateway authorization before any action that creates financial, custody, market, or securities exposure.

The Market & Asset Lab is not authorized by this charter to operate as a broker, exchange, investment adviser, custodian, money transmitter, securities issuer, or pooled investment vehicle.

---

## A2. Ownership and Authority

### Product owner
Hip-Hop Peoples DAO LLC owns the District 05 product/governance surface unless a later written resolution assigns a specific function to another entity.

### System supervisor
Stevie AI Native Market Sentinel may:
- collect permitted market and contract data;
- assign provisional PASS / WATCH / HOLD / ESCALATE states;
- identify missing evidence;
- route proposals to the correct human authority;
- maintain evidence references and decision history.

Stevie AI Native Market Sentinel may not:
- execute purchases, swaps, sales, bridges, liquidity additions, or withdrawals;
- sign transactions with an ecosystem treasury wallet;
- move participant or customer funds;
- approve a Class D asset;
- determine that an offering is legally compliant;
- override a human HOLD;
- infer authority from possession of credentials or wallet access.

### Human Gateway
A named human authority must approve any action that:
- creates an onchain asset;
- exposes an ecosystem entity to payment, custody, liquidity, investment, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations;
- changes an asset classification;
- activates a smart contract with economic rights;
- establishes a liquidity pool;
- links an asset to revenue, royalties, ownership, debt, profit sharing, yield, or appreciation claims.

No Human Gateway approval is valid without a corresponding evidence record.

## A3. Freedom Notes Firewall

Freedom Notes™ remain governed by their existing policy:

- non-monetary;
- non-transferable;
- not redeemable for cash;
- not ownership;
- not debt;
- not profit participation;
- not an investment;
- not a cryptocurrency or tradeable token.

District 05 must maintain a **separate ledger and policy lane** from Freedom Notes.

District 05 may read an approved participation status or eligibility output from the Freedom Notes system where policy permits, but it may not:

- convert Freedom Notes into cash, tokens, stablecoins, securities, gift cards, stored value, discounts with a fixed exchange rate, or marketable assets;
- create a redemption schedule between Freedom Notes and any financial asset;
- use Freedom Notes balances as collateral;
- represent Freedom Notes as appreciating in value;
- import participant-level Freedom Notes records into a public chain or public repository.

Any future proposal to change one of those restrictions must be treated as a new governance and legal matter; it is not authorized through ordinary District 05 administration.

## A4. Asset Classification Test

Every proposed asset must be classified before any contract deployment, mint, public sale, transfer feature, market listing, or liquidity action.

### Step 1 — Identify the right or function

Record exactly what the holder receives.

Examples:
- proof that an event occurred;
- access to a service or event;
- credential or membership status;
- right to a product;
- right to repayment;
- right to revenue or royalties;
- equity or governance tied to economic value;
- expectation of yield, profit, appreciation, or resale.

### Step 2 — Economic-interest screen

A proposal enters the **Class D legal gate** if any of the following are present or reasonably contemplated:

- ownership or equity;
- debt or repayment obligation;
- revenue share;
- royalty share;
- profit share;
- yield, interest, staking return, dividend, or passive financial return;
- pooled capital;
- fractional ownership of a real-world asset;
- tokenization of a security or financial claim;
- promises or marketing centered on price appreciation or resale profit;
- issuer-supported secondary-market liquidity for an economically participatory instrument;
- redemption into cash or another financial asset where the structure may create stored-value, payment, money-transmission, or securities implications.

Presence of one of these factors does not by itself determine final legal status. It **does** require the proposal to stop at the Class D gate until reviewed.

### Step 3 — Transferability screen

For any transferable asset, document:

- why transferability is necessary;
- who may transfer;
- transfer restrictions;
- whether a secondary market is contemplated;
- whether the issuer or ecosystem will provide or incentivize liquidity;
- whether identity/KYC/AML controls are required;
- tax/accounting treatment;
- consumer-protection implications.

Transferability is never enabled solely because the blockchain technically permits it.

### Step 4 — Marketing screen

Marketing language must be reviewed for claims involving:

- guaranteed return;
- appreciation;
- “early investor” status;
- “next XRP” or similar comparisons;
- passive income;
- yield;
- profit from ecosystem efforts;
- scarcity intended primarily to induce investment demand.

Any such language triggers HOLD pending review.

### Step 5 — Written legal disposition

Class D may move forward only after a written disposition identifies, as applicable:

- responsible legal entity / issuer;
- instrument classification;
- offering or exemption pathway;
- permitted purchaser/user population;
- jurisdictional restrictions;
- KYC/AML/sanctions requirements;
- custody model;
- transfer restrictions;
- venue/market structure;
- tax treatment;
- accounting treatment;
- required disclosures;
- required contracts;
- required approvals.

“Permissionless deployment” is not a substitute for a written disposition.

## A5. Provisional Class Structure

### Class A — Proof / Attestation
Examples:
- policy hash;
- credential proof;
- completion receipt;
- snapshot fingerprint.

Default posture:
- non-transferable or hash-only;
- no financial rights;
- no private data onchain.

### Class B — Utility / Access
Examples:
- membership access;
- event ticket;
- studio entitlement;
- digital access credential.

Default posture:
- use-based;
- no promised profit;
- no passive yield;
- no ownership/revenue rights;
- transferability disabled unless justified and approved.

### Class C — Commerce
Examples:
- payment for goods/services;
- USDC/Base Pay settlement;
- merchant receipt.

Default posture:
- payment only;
- avoid HHP custody of user funds;
- reconcile to the correct operating entity;
- maintain refund and accounting evidence.

### Class D — Financial / RWA / Investment-Like
Examples:
- equity;
- debt;
- film revenue participation;
- royalties;
- real-estate interests;
- fractionalized assets;
- pooled investment interests;
- transferable profit-sharing instruments.

Default posture:
**HOLD until written legal/compliance disposition and Human Gateway approval.**

## A6. Escalation Matrix

### PASS — Observe
Used when:
- data provenance is sufficient;
- no prohibited action is requested;
- proposal remains within approved Class A/B/C controls.

Authority:
- Sentinel may assign provisionally;
- human review may sample or confirm under operating policy.

### WATCH
Used when:
- information is incomplete;
- contract controls are unusual;
- market/liquidity data is weak;
- identity/source evidence needs strengthening;
- transferability or economic rights are ambiguous.

Authority:
- Sentinel may assign;
- no execution follows WATCH status.

### HOLD
Used when:
- Class D factor appears;
- legal/entity authority is unresolved;
- PII/PHI exposure is possible;
- custody or treasury movement is requested;
- smart-contract control is unclear;
- sanctions/scam/security concern is material;
- data provenance is insufficient for action.

Authority:
- only the designated Human Gateway or required specialist process may clear the HOLD.

### ESCALATE
Used when:
- outside counsel, accounting/tax, compliance, security, treasury, insurance, or another named specialist must decide;
- the proposal crosses entity or jurisdictional boundaries;
- a prior approved classification no longer matches actual functionality.

## A7. Reconciliation and Evidence

Every approved onchain or payment event must reconcile to an internal record containing:

- internal transaction/approval ID;
- responsible entity;
- asset or offer ID;
- classification;
- approving human;
- approval timestamp;
- source/evidence references;
- wallet/account role;
- transaction hash where applicable;
- amount and denomination where applicable;
- accounting destination;
- exception notes;
- reversal/refund status where applicable.

No file / no evidence means no final reconciliation.

Public chain references may prove that an event occurred, but they are not the private system of record.

## A8. Data Boundary

District 05 follows the existing routing rule:

**private records stay private; public systems show structures, proofs, and approved summaries.**

Never place onchain or in public GitHub:

- names tied to protected participant records;
- addresses;
- medical or behavioral-health information;
- court/case details;
- internal eligibility determinations;
- participant-specific Freedom Notes balances;
- private treasury keys;
- seed phrases;
- private compliance evidence;
- confidential contracts unless specifically authorized for publication.

Public identifiers must not be reversible into protected participant records without access to the authorized private system.

## A9. Treasury and Execution Boundary

Release 1 is **research and verification first**.

Not authorized in Release 1:

- autonomous treasury trading;
- auto-swaps;
- copy trading;
- pooled user funds;
- discretionary investment management;
- HHP custody of user crypto;
- HHP-issued investment token;
- HHP-funded liquidity pool for a Class D asset;
- automated bridging of treasury assets.

Any later execution function requires a separate authority matrix and wallet-control policy.

---

## A10. Entity Assignment Rule

District 05 itself does not decide which ecosystem entity becomes issuer, merchant, licensor, rights owner, payment recipient, or treasury owner.

Before a financially significant asset or transaction is activated, the internal record must identify:

- legal entity;
- authority source;
- beneficial/economic rights;
- bank/wallet ownership;
- accounting treatment;
- contractual counterparty;
- responsible signer.

If any of those are unresolved, status is HOLD.

This rule preserves the existing firewalls between community/governance, media, workforce, clinical/support, fiscal-sponsor, and commercial lanes.

---

## A11. Change Control

A material change includes:

- making a non-transferable asset transferable;
- adding redemption;
- adding yield or rewards with financial value;
- creating a secondary market;
- introducing pooled funds;
- adding leverage/borrowing;
- connecting a treasury wallet;
- changing issuer/entity;
- changing user population or jurisdiction;
- linking Freedom Notes to monetary value.

Material changes require:
1. new classification;
2. new evidence review;
3. Human Gateway approval;
4. specialist/legal review where the Class D screen is triggered;
5. versioned governance record.

---

## A12. Controlling Governance Principle

**Freedom Notes recognize participation. District 05 verifies assets and markets. Neither function may silently become an investment product. Financial rights require a separate entity, legal pathway, evidence file, and Human Gateway decision before activation.**

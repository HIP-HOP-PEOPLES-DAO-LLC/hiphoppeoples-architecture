Trust & Disclosure

MASPT provides administrative support, compliance coordination, and case monitoring services in connection with bail bond financing. All bail bonds are written by independently licensed bail bond agents under surety carrier authority. MASPT is not a surety insurer. Services are designed to support court compliance through communication, reminders, and structured engagement. No outcome is guaranteed, and all defendants remain responsible for meeting court obligations.

Program Disclosure

Participation in the Bail Back Initiative™ and Work Release Program is voluntary and not required as a condition of bail or release. All program activities operate independently of bail bond agreements. Participation is based on availability, eligibility, and program capacity. Compensation or opportunities provided through program participation are not connected to bail bond premiums or the cost of bail. Participation does not influence legal outcomes, court decisions, or case status.
